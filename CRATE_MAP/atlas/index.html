WHAT THIS VERSION KNOWS

Coverage, without the fog.

The global base map covers the world. The researched histories in this pilot concern the US and Europe, with a Canadian performance. Unmarked places mean “not researched here”, never “no musical history”.

For the complete personal map, the next evidence needed is the ordered contents of the other crates and your actual play-history exports. Account-wide sync, public profiles and other users’ libraries are not implemented.

Import a CRATE JUICE list or catalogue JSON export with tracks, artist, title and source URLs. This adds searchable records on this browser only; it does not upload files or overwrite the canonical catalogue.

CRATE JUICE atlas exports can also restore your explored branches. New recordings stay marked “history pending”; live exploration is available without treating its results as reviewed history. Use Import crates for ordered SoundCloud capture and full library exports. Raw listening-history formats still need separate adapters.
